# Streamer DAC / Power Supply Enclosure Panels and Dimension Drawings

This document contains the panel drawings and dimensions for the three enclosures that make up the Streamer DAC:

- **The main Streamer DAC enclosure** – the outer case with the front panel, rear panel, floor, top cover and side panels.
- **The inner enclosure** – a smaller case that sits inside the main enclosure and holds the control electronics (Raspberry Pi 4 and control board), shielded from the power supply.
- **The power supply enclosure** – a separate case that houses the internal power supply.

All drawings were created in Solid Edge. Dimensions are in millimetres unless stated otherwise. For how the parts are put together, see [Component-Assemblies.md](Component-Assemblies.md).

## DAC Inner Case Dimensions and Assembly

The inner case is built from a front panel, a back panel, two side panels with an internal power supply shield, a base, and a shield for the Raspberry Pi 4 and control electronics. The front and back panels are each 380 mm long and 85 mm high.

### Inner Case Front Panel

Carries the mounting holes, the round holes for the front controls and the cut-outs for the display connection. The hole positions and sizes are listed in the table on the drawing.

![Inner Case Front Panel](Images/Inner%20case%20front.png)

### Inner Case Back Panel

Has three slots cut into its lower edge: one for the ribbon cable from the reclocker to the monitor Pi, one for the HDMI and USB cables, and one for the buttons and LEDs. The gaps around the cables can be sealed with copper tape to keep the case shielded.

![Inner Case Back Panel](Images/Inner%20case%20Back%20Panel.png)
