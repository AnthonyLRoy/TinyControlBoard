### Powering the Player on and off

#### <span class="smallcaps">Turning On</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">The power button \[13\] is tucked underneath
the cabinet, along the front edge. A short press will wake the player
from standby. </span><span class="smallcaps">Below is what you will see
for normal operation</span>**

1.  **<span class="smallcaps">The Power LED \[18\] flashes red.</span>**

2.  **<span class="smallcaps">Button LEDs \[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 10\]
    illuminate blue.</span>**

3.  **<span class="smallcaps">As the system initializes, the button LEDs
    gradually turn off.</span>**

4.  **<span class="smallcaps">When startup is complete:</span>**

5.  **<span class="smallcaps">All button LEDs are off.</span>**

6.  **<span class="smallcaps">The Power LED \[18\] changes to solid
    blue.</span>**

7.  **<span class="smallcaps">The DAC is now ready for use.</span>**

If there is an error during the turn on process the one or more of the
lights will flash. See the Errors section at the end of this document.

#### <span class="smallcaps">Putting the Player to Sleep (Standby)</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">When you have finished listening and want to
put the player into standby, give the power button \[13\] a short press.
The player will save </span><span class="smallcaps">the current
brightness level of the monitor and
buttons,</span><span class="smallcaps">
</span><span class="smallcaps">then
</span><span class="smallcaps">go</span><span class="smallcaps"> into a
low-power sleep mode. The main display will turn off, and the system
will be ready to wake up again quickly the next time you press the
button.</span><span class="smallcaps"> The
DAC</span><span class="smallcaps">’</span><span class="smallcaps">s and
the output stage will be kept on to keep them in the optimal operating
temperature.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Deep Sleep</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">If you plan to leave the player unused for a
longer period, you can put it into a deeper sleep mode that uses even
less power. To do this, press and hold the power button \[13\] for about
</span><span class="smallcaps">four</span><span class="smallcaps">
seconds, then release. The player will go through a proper shutdown
sequence before entering deep sleep. To use the player again after a
deep sleep, press the power button as
normal.</span><span class="smallcaps"> If you are going away for a
couple of days it is best to switch the power off completely, see power
supply secton.</span>**

## Rear Panel Overview

**<span class="smallcaps">Before connecting your DAC, take a few moments
to familiarise yourself with the rear panel. Every connector has been
carefully designed to provide reliable operation while offering maximum
flexibility when integrating the DAC into your audio system.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Whether you are connecting the external power
supply, digital music sources, your home network or your amplifier, the
following sections explain the purpose of each connection and when you
would typically use it.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">If you are new to standalone DACs, don't worry
if some of the connection names are unfamiliar. Most users will only use
a small number of these connections during normal operation.</span>**

### Rear Panel Layout

**<span class="smallcaps">The rear panel is divided into six functional
areas.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Grounding & Control</span>

Connections used for system grounding and communication with the
external power supply.

#### <span class="smallcaps">DC Power Inputs</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">Dedicated power connections supplying the DAC
from the external power supply.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Digital Audio Inputs</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">Connect digital music sources using
I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S, USB
Audio, Optical (TOSLINK) or Coaxial S/PDIF.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Network & Expansion</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">Ethernet networking, firmware updates and USB
storage support.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Analogue Audio Outputs</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">Single-ended RCA and balanced XLR outputs for
connection to amplifiers or pre-amplifiers.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Ground Lift Controls</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">Optional switches that can help eliminate hum
caused by ground loops in certain audio systems.</span>**

### <span class="smallcaps">Connector Descriptions</span>

#### <span class="smallcaps">1 & 3 </span><span class="smallcaps">–</span><span class="smallcaps"> Chassis Earth Connections</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">These banana socket terminals connect the DAC
chassis to the earth connection on the external power supply.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">For most installations, no adjustment is
required. However, if unwanted hum or electrical noise is present, these
connections can help optimise system grounding and reduce
interference.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Note</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">In most systems these connections will already
be correctly configured and require no further adjustment.</span>**

#### 2 – Power Supply Control Connection

**<span class="smallcaps">This connector links the DAC directly to the
external power supply.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">It carries the control signals required to
manage the internal power rails and enables features such as
</span><span class="smallcaps">Sleep</span><span class="smallcaps"> and
</span><span class="smallcaps">Deep Sleep</span><span class="smallcaps">
mode. This ensures both units power up and shut down in the correct
sequence.</span>**

#### 4 & 5 – DC Power Inputs

**<span class="smallcaps">These connectors supply the main DC power from
the external power supply to the DAC.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Each connector is uniquely keyed and can only
be inserted in the correct orientation, preventing accidental incorrect
connection.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Once connected, the locking mechanism ensures
a secure and reliable electrical connection.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Important</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Never force a connector into place. If
correctly aligned, each connector will fit easily.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">6 </span><span class="smallcaps">–</span><span class="smallcaps"> Firmware Update Port</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">This USB connection is reserved exclusively
for firmware updates.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">When new firmware becomes available, this port
is used to update the DAC's internal software.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Important</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">This port is
</span><span class="smallcaps">not</span><span class="smallcaps">
intended for music playback or USB storage devices.</span>**

#### 7 – USB Storage Port

**<span class="smallcaps">This USB port allows music to be played
directly from compatible USB storage devices.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Supported devices include:</span>**

- **<span class="smallcaps">USB flash drives </span>**

- **<span class="smallcaps">Externally powered USB hard drives </span>**

- **<span class="smallcaps">Compatible USB solid-state drives (SSDs)
  </span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Once connected, the storage device can be
selected from the DAC's user interface.</span>**

#### 8 – I²S Digital Audio Input (HDMI Connector)

**<span class="smallcaps">Although this connector uses a standard
HDMI-style socket, </span><span class="smallcaps">it does not carry HDMI
video signals</span><span class="smallcaps">.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Instead, it provides an
</span><span class="smallcaps">I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S
digital audio input</span><span class="smallcaps">, offering one of the
highest-quality methods of transferring digital audio between compatible
source devices and the DAC.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Unlike USB or S/PDIF,
I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S
carries the audio data and clock signals separately, reducing the amount
of clock recovery required within the DAC.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Important</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S
is </span><span class="smallcaps">not</span><span class="smallcaps"> an
industry-standard interface. Different manufacturers use different
signal assignments (pinouts).</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">This DAC is designed for equipment using the
</span><span class="smallcaps">PS Audio
I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S
pinout</span><span class="smallcaps">. Before connecting an
I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S
source, ensure that it is compatible with the PS Audio
standard.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Tip</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">If your source device provides both USB Audio
and I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S
outputs, either connection can provide excellent sound quality. Many
enthusiasts prefer
I</span><span class="smallcaps">²</span><span class="smallcaps">S
because of its dedicated clock and data signals.</span>**

#### 9 – USB Audio Input

#### <span class="smallcaps">Earth Connections \[1 and 3\]</span>

during playback, these connections may help improve system grounding and
reduce interference.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">It is recommended to connect one of these
connections to the Streamer DAC by default</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Power Supply Control</span><span class="smallcaps"> Connection</span><span class="smallcaps"> \[2\]</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">This connection links directly to the
accompanying power supply and carries the control signals used to manage
the internal power rails.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">It is used when placing the DAC into
</span><span class="smallcaps">Sleep</span><span class="smallcaps"> or
</span><span class="smallcaps">Deep Sleep</span><span class="smallcaps">
mode, allowing the system to power down and wake up correctly.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Main Power Connections \[</span><span class="smallcaps">9</span><span class="smallcaps"> and </span><span class="smallcaps">10</span><span class="smallcaps">\]</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">These connectors provide the main DC power
feed from the external power supply to the DAC.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">The connectors are keyed differently, so they
can only be connected in the correct orientation. This prevents
accidental misconnection and makes installation
straightforward.</span>**

#### <span class="smallcaps">Fuses \[4,5,6,7\]</span>

**<span class="smallcaps">These output fuses provide protection for the
connected equipment and the power supply outputs in the event of an
electrical fault or excessive current draw.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Under normal operation, these fuses should not
require replacement. However, if the power supply is switched on and the
</span><span class="smallcaps">5V UcPure</span><span class="smallcaps">
or </span><span class="smallcaps">3.3V
UcPure</span><span class="smallcaps"> status LEDs on the front panel do
not illuminate, it is possible that one or more output fuses may have
operated (blown).</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Before replacing any fuse, it is recommended
to identify and correct the cause of the fault, as repeated fuse failure
may indicate an issue with the connected equipment or wiring.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Warning:</span>**\
**<span class="smallcaps">Always switch the power supply off and
disconnect the mains power lead before inspecting or replacing any fuse.
Failure to do so may expose hazardous voltages and could result in
electric shock or damage to the equipment.</span>**

**<span class="smallcaps">Only replace fuses with the
</span><span class="smallcaps">same type and current
rating</span><span class="smallcaps"> as originally specified. Using an
incorrect fuse may compromise safety and damage the power supply or
connected devices.</span>**
